> **The baseline result is that it does not start.** vLLM cannot reserve a 128K
> context window in 7.38 GiB of KV cache. Recorded as `"startup": "FAILED"`, not
> retried until something succeeded. `baseline-servable` - the single minimal
> change required to serve - is the reference point everything is measured
> against.

## Honest gaps

- **Output quality was not measured.** INT4 is the largest lever here and the only
  change that can alter what the model says. A quality evaluation on the
  customer's own prompts belongs beside these numbers before production use.
- **`nvlink-off` is a lower bound**, not a simulation of PCIe-attached A100s.
  Disabling peer-to-peer forces host-memory staging, which is worse than PCIe
  P2P. It brackets the answer rather than reproducing it.
- **One workload shape.** 512-token prompts, 256-token outputs. Long-context and
  long-generation workloads would likely shift the recommendation.
- **One hardware configuration.** No claim about how this transfers to H100s,
  where FP8 compute exists and the arithmetic changes.
